# Support

- **Questions about Amtsorakel or hexese.com** (not about the code): write to
  [info@hexese.com](mailto:info@hexese.com). Amtsorakel also answers at
  [info@amtsorakel.de](mailto:info@amtsorakel.de).
- **Security issues:** never as an issue — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Bugs and plans inside a repository** you have access to: open an issue with
  one of the templates (Bug, Feature, Task).

Amtsorakel explains and quotes official letters; it gives no legal advice
(§ 2 RDG), and neither does support.
